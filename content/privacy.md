This policy covers the website `www.victorexplore.com` and the 1000 Cuts game at `1000cuts.victorexplore.com`. The LastCig app has [its own policy](/lastcig/privacy/).

## This website

- The blog has no accounts. It does not ask for your name or email address.
- One page, [Anchor](/anchor/), has a waitlist form. If you fill it in, your email address goes to Kit, the email service that keeps the list. That page has [its own policy](/anchor/privacy/).
- GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub receives technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser loads a page. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- Pages with maths load a script from the jsDelivr network. jsDelivr receives your IP address when that occurs.
- Your browser keeps two small settings on your device: the light or dark theme, and the menu position. These do not leave your device.

## The 1000 Cuts game

You can play without giving any data. The game stores data only when you claim a place on the leaderboard.

**What the game stores on its server**

- The name that you type.
- The photo that you add, if you add one. The game makes it small (256 pixels) before it sends it.
- Your country. The game suggests a country from your network address, and you can change it before you save. The game does not store your IP address.
- Your best score for each object, and the time of that score.
- A random player ID. The server keeps only a hash (a one-way code) of the secret token that goes with it.

**What is public**

Your name, photo, country and scores show on the public leaderboard and on challenge links that you share.

**What stays in your browser**

Your player ID and token, your best scores, your profile, the sound setting and the last object that you cut. Clear the site data in your browser to remove them.

**Other services**

- Cloudflare hosts the game and its database. Cloudflare receives technical data, such as your IP address. See the [Cloudflare privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- The game loads its font from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when that occurs.

**How to remove your data**

Send an email to avinashbarak7@gmail.com with your leaderboard name and country. We then delete your name, photo and scores.

For more on how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children

The website and the game are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them. If a child put a name or photo on the leaderboard, write to us and we delete it.

## What we do not do
